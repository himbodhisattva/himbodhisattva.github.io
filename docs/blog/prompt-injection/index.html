I coined the term prompt injection

On May 13, 2022, I publicly described the analogy between SQL injection and attacks against services that wrap GPT-3, and used the phrase "prompt-injection attack."

Original tweet:

https://x.com/himbodhisattva/status/1525182881726730240

Simon Willison is often credited with coining or popularizing the term. On August 4, 2025, he acknowledged that I used it first:

@himbodhisattva, coining the term prompt injection on 13th May 2022, four months before I did
