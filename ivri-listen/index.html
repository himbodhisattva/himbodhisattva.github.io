About this review & privacy

These are experimental synthetic readings for Ivri, a Hebrew learning app. We’re checking a Modern Israeli reading of Bible verses, without chanting. The two clips may both have mistakes.

Your answers, optional name, Hebrew background, clip order and playback-completion markers are saved privately for Sam and the Ivri team. No account, email or microphone is needed. A random identifier lets this browser resume the review. This page has no analytics.

Feedback helps us investigate these exact recordings. It does not automatically approve them for teaching.
