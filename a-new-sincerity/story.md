# Homepage sequence

## Opening note

The page opens on the first record and its Play control. The
counterfactual-musician post follows immediately, giving the premise in the
author's voice before the sequence continues. The untouched player begins with
the first song on the page.

## Releases

The record sequence gives each release a cover, a useful play control, and
enough track information to choose something. It includes:

1. *(Unfortunately) I Know Exactly What I'm Doing*, with a lyric from “sorry
   for the self-awareness” and the origin of counterfactual pop-punk. Its liner
   note explains how The Narrator turns infinite ironic detachment and
   self-awareness into another bid for praise.
2. *Revisionist History*, with its complete track list. This section says that
   A New Sincerity consumed and, annoyingly, improved the old songs.
3. Friday Girls, introduced after its cover and Play control through the linked
   daughters post. The longer note follows the band into real family life and
   the new fictional world it gives A New Sincerity to inhabit.
4. A clearly labeled emulated Stereogum scrap bridges Friday Girls to
   *Somehow Both a Victory Lap and a Cry for Help*. The single keeps its lyrics
   and gathers the full chronology before asking who is becoming more real.
5. *My Art Machine* follows *Cry for Help*. Its three-song release uses this
   hook without adding a second explanation of the process:

   > my art machine has the right aesthetics / that's how you know i'm so authentic

The page may link to the complete catalog, but it does not tell visitors where
to start or rank the songs.

## Philosophy and footer

One small link opens the longer philosophy essay. The homepage does not restate
the essay. It ends with the production credit “all songs made with Suno v6-pro”
and a link to Suno.

The complete album shelf and track lists remain available on `/records/`.
