# A New Sincerity

## somehow both a victory lap and a cry for help

> this is getting a bit disorienting... I made a counterfactual me who stayed an
> emo/posthardcore musician, then had him remix my (real! actually recorded)
> old songs

The album came first; the remixes followed.

## The first experiment

*(Unfortunately) I Know Exactly What I'm Doing* came first: what would
counterfactual me be writing if I were still making pop-punk? Hyperpop-punk,
sincerity and self-absorption cranked up.

Was that a dead end, or could you keep going?

## Keep going

So I had A New Sincerity remake songs I wrote and recorded with Lock Haven and
The System of the World.

For “Like They Do In The Movies,” the site places the Lock Haven recording and
the A New Sincerity remake together. The remake brings the high guitar harmonies
forward and gives the chorus room to open.

## The review became the next song

I asked for an emulated Stereogum review of *(unfortunately)*. It found the
potential emotional center of the album. I sampled the review, answered it, and
made the answer into the next song.

> And every time he gets unnerved, he converts the experience into an artistic
> achievement, thereby making the thing unnerving him even more successful.

The song answers, “Well, fuck. That's pretty good.”

The sequence is straightforward: write the album, ask for the review, sample
the review, turn it into a chorus, and make the concern more successful.

## Liner notes

The site keeps four notes in one pull-out sleeve.

### The counterfactual

The old recordings are real. The singer on the remakes is generated. I kept the
words and parts, then used A New Sincerity to continue the career I did not
have.

### “Fictional”

I wrote the Friday Girls songs with AI, chose the performances, and produced the
album. The voices are generated. My family listens to the finished songs.

### The five percent

There is a small crush on the fictional band, plus some envy of how easily they
sing happy songs. That attachment partly explains why I kept replaying the album.

### The control loop

I asked AI to review the album, sampled a sentence from the review in the next
track, and built this page around that track.
